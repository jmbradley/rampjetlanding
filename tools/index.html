Tool 01

The Founder Absence Checklist

Most owners can name the one thing that stops when they're away. The second and third take longer to produce, and those are usually the ones that cost money.

Three weeks is the useful test. A week can be absorbed by one person texting you. Three is long enough that a quote goes stale, a customer calls twice, and a job ships without anyone invoicing it.

Everything you know that isn't written down is worth exactly nothing to whoever comes next.
